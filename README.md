# 🎬 CINEVERSE — Your Universe of Cinema

**An AI-powered movie discovery, recommendation, and community platform.**

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-Visit%20CINEVERSE-8B5CF6?style=for-the-badge)](https://cineverse-usrj.hatchable.site)

CINEVERSE brings movie discovery, AI-powered recommendations, personal movie tracking, reviews, quizzes, and cinephile community features together in one platform.

Explore films, discover your next favourite movie, track your cinema journey, and connect with other movie enthusiasts.

## ✨ Features

### 1. 🔎 Movie Discovery
- Explore trending, popular, top-rated, and upcoming movies.
- Search for movies and explore genres.
- Discover films using real movie database information.

### 2. 🤖 CineAI — AI Movie Assistant
- Get movie recommendations based on your mood and preferences.
- Discover similar movies.
- Explore AI-generated movie suggestions grounded in movie database results.

### 3. 🎥 Movie Explorer
- Explore movie information, descriptions, posters, and cast details.
- Discover related films.
- Access movie information from the TMDB database.

### 4. ⭐ Reviews & Ratings
- Write and manage movie reviews.
- Rate movies out of 10.
- Mark reviews containing spoilers.
- Like helpful reviews and explore community ratings.

### 5. 🎞️ My Cinema
- Maintain your personal movie library.
- Track watched movies and watchlists.
- Create custom movie collections.
- Save personal notes and watched dates.

### 6. 📊 Cinema Statistics
- Track your watched movies and reviews.
- Explore your favourite genres and directors.
- View your movie-watching activity and taste profile.

### 7. 🧠 CineQuiz
- Test your movie knowledge.
- Play Mixed Quiz, Scene Clue, Release Year, and Plot Challenge modes.
- Get timed questions, scores, and answer feedback.

### 8. 🌍 CineCommunity
- Discover other movie enthusiasts.
- Create and customise your profile.
- Follow other users.
- Explore community reviews and activity.

5. Deploy the application through Hatchable.

Refer to the Hatchable documentation for platform-specific setup instructions.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of CINEVERSE is to create a unified cinema platform that combines traditional movie discovery with AI-powered recommendations and social features.

Rather than switching between multiple platforms, users can discover movies, organise their watchlists, share reviews, test their knowledge, and explore their personal cinema statistics in one place.

## 👨‍💻 Development

CINEVERSE was built as a college portfolio project using an AI-assisted development workflow. The project involved feature planning, frontend customisation, API integration, database functionality, testing, debugging, and deployment.
